Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Krish Raut ("I", "me", or "my") handles information when you visit this portfolio and its browser games. If you have a question or privacy request, use the contact details at the end of this page.

Information used by the games

You can play the games without signing in. Context Guess compares your guesses with a vocabulary dataset downloaded to your browser; guesses are not submitted to the site's player database. Number Guess downloads the Python runtime from jsDelivr only when you open that game.

If you choose Google sign-in for the shared leaderboard, Firebase Authentication processes your Google account information to authenticate you. The Python score service verifies your sign-in and stores an account identifier, your chosen player name, and game information such as points, wins, games played, and score records in its database. The production database is hosted separately from this static website. Your email address is not displayed on the public leaderboard. Player names and leaderboard figures are public; do not choose a name or share information you do not want shown publicly.

Google and Firebase handle authentication information under their own privacy terms. Read the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy and security information.

Advertising, cookies, and similar technologies

If Google AdSense ads are served on this site, Google and third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. These technologies may use information about your visit to this site or other sites to provide personalized advertising, where permitted. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on a user's visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also learn about Google's advertising technologies at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, review the ad technology partners selected for this site in AdSense, and review additional opt-out choices at aboutads.info. Your browser also provides controls for cookies and site data.

Where consent is required for advertising, a Google-certified consent management platform must be configured before ads are enabled for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and any other region where applicable law or Google policy requires it. When a consent message is shown, use its controls to make or change your choices. You can also manage personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings.

Other service providers

This site uses GitHub Pages and Vercel for hosting, Google Fonts for typography, jsDelivr to load the optional Python runtime, Firebase Authentication for optional Google sign-in, and a Python score service for the leaderboard. If ads are served, Google and its advertising partners also process information as described above. These services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and request data when your device loads their resources. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies. External links on this site take you to services that I do not operate.

Messages and privacy requests

If you contact me by email or WhatsApp, the information you send is handled by those services and is used to respond to you. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of a player profile's information by contacting me. I may need enough information to verify and complete the request.

Children

This site is a personal portfolio and collection of browser games, and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a signed-in player profile, contact me so I can review the request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or its services change. The latest version and its update date will be posted on this page.
